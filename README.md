# Lockbox

Our phones and electronic devices have become a huge part of our lives, so much so that sometimes they prove to distract us from doing what we need to do as a means of procrastination. 

Our idea was to make a safe box to keep those devices in for an adjustable period of time so that you can focus on your work without getting distracted. This is an especially prevalent issue 
within the younger generations and so our solution is aimed specifically at students who are struggling to tackle this issue.
